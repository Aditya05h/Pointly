PROCESS

Up and running
in four steps.

01

Download & Install

Click Download for Windows. Run Pointly-Setup.exe and follow the 2-step wizard. No admin rights needed for basic use. Takes under a minute.

02

Allow microphone access for voice. All processing runs locally — nothing leaves your device.

03

Pick Voice or Text from the system tray. You can switch modes at any time — even mid-conversation.

Pointly lives quietly in your tray. Wake it with Ctrl+Alt+Space, or just say It's always ready when you are.

Pointly is our answer. An AI assistant that meets you wherever you are — through voice or text — in your language, on your terms. We believe human-computer interaction should be as natural as a conversation.
