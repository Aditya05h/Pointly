PROCESS

Up and running
in four steps.

01

Download & Install

Click Download for Windows. Run Pointly-Setup.exe and follow the 2-step wizard. No admin rights needed for basic use. Takes under a minute.

02

Grant Permissions

Allow microphone access for voice, and webcam access for sign language and air-cursor modes. All processing runs locally — nothing leaves your device.

03

Choose Your Mode

Pick Voice, Sign, Air-Cursor, or Text from the system tray. You can switch modes at any time — even mid-conversation.

04

Start Interacting

Pointly lives quietly in your tray. Wake it with Ctrl+Shift+P, a hand gesture, or just say "Hey Pointly". It's always ready when you are.

ABOUT

Why we built
Pointly.

Billions of people use computers every day — yet the interface has barely changed in 40 years. Keyboards and mice still dominate, locking out millions of users with motor disabilities, speech impairments, or language barriers.

Pointly is our answer. An AI assistant that meets you wherever you are — through voice, sign, gesture, or text — in your language, on your terms. We believe human-computer interaction should be as natural as a conversation.
